Lasagna

Description

A classic Italian baked pasta dish made with layers of noodles, meat sauce, and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Cook the ground beef and mix with marinara sauce.
  3. Layer noodles, meat sauce, and cheeses in a baking dish.
  4. Repeat the layers, finishing with cheese on top.
  5. Bake for 45 minutes until bubbly.
