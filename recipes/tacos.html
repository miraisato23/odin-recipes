Tacos

Description

Classic Mexican tacos with seasoned beef, fresh toppings, and a warm tortilla.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 8 small tortillas
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • 1 tomato, diced
  • Sour cream, to taste

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef in a pan over medium heat until browned.
  2. Add taco seasoning and water as directed on the packet, simmer until thickened.
  3. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan or microwave.
  4. Fill each tortilla with beef, then top with lettuce, cheese, and tomato.
  5. Add sour cream on top and serve.
